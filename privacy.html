Last updated: September 3, 2026

Membership agreement Terms of service Privacy policy

This document is available in English. The English version governs.

This Privacy Policy explains how Best Vision Accounting (“Best Vision”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit the IRS Protect website, enroll in a membership, contact us or submit an IRS notice (together, the “Services”). Tax information you provide is also protected by the confidentiality rules that apply to tax return preparers, including Internal Revenue Code section 7216.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enrollment: name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, filing status and the membership you choose.
  • Contact requests: name, email address, phone number, topic and the content of your message.
  • Notice submissions: name, contact details, member ID, notice number and date, tax year, your notes, and the documents you upload. IRS notices and tax returns may contain sensitive information such as your Social Security number, income and account details.
  • Payments: payment is handled by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and the last digits of your card, but we do not receive or store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Language preference: the Site stores your English/Spanish choice in your browser’s local storage so the Site remembers it. This is not shared with us.
  • Server logs and hosting: our hosting provider may record IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps for security and operations.
  • Web fonts: the Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested.

The Site does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To set up, bill and service your membership and verify eligibility of IRS matters;
  • To review IRS notices, prepare responses and represent you before the IRS as you authorize;
  • To respond to your questions and requests;
  • To send transactional messages about your membership, such as confirmations, billing notices and updates on your matter;
  • To operate, secure and improve the Services and comply with legal and professional obligations.

We use your tax return information only as permitted by law and your authorization, and we do not use it for marketing unrelated products without your consent.

3. How we share information

  • The IRS: as necessary to respond to a notice or represent you, and only as you authorize (for example through Form 2848 or Form 8821).
  • Service providers that help us run the Services, such as payment processing, form delivery and email, document storage and website hosting. They may use information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Best Vision, our members or others.
  • Business transfers: if Best Vision is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred to the successor, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Retention

We keep membership and matter records for as long as your membership is active and afterwards as required by tax practitioner record-keeping rules and applicable law, generally at least three years after the matter closes. Contact messages are kept as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards. You may ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the Site and access controls for documents. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send Social Security numbers or full account numbers in the free-text message fields; upload the notice itself instead, or share sensitive details with the team directly.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You may access, correct or update your membership information by contacting us.
  • You may ask us to delete information we are not legally required to retain.
  • You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time; we will still send messages needed to service your membership.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know what information we hold and to request deletion. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

7. Children

The Services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Policy on this page with a new “Last updated” date and, for material changes, notify members by email.
