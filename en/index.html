How coverage works

Clear rules, taken straight from the membership agreement.

Coverage rules

  1. 1
    The notice date decidesA matter is eligible only if your membership was active and in good standing on the date printed on the first IRS notice. Coverage is not retroactive.
  2. 2
    Future tax years includedAs long as your membership stays active, there is no fixed limit on the number of future tax years that may qualify.
  3. 3
    Stay active for the whole matterYou must remain active and in good standing until Best Vision determines its services are complete. Reactivating a membership does not restore coverage for matters that arose while it was inactive.
  4. 4
    Who represents youAn Enrolled Agent (EA), CPA, attorney, or other professional authorized to represent you before the IRS.

What’s not covered

General exclusions under the membership agreement:

  • Fraud or intentional tax evasion
  • Criminal tax investigations
  • IRS notices or matters dated before your enrollment date
  • False or intentionally incomplete tax returns
  • Matters resulting from information intentionally concealed or withheld
  • Court or U.S. Tax Court representation
  • Matters specifically excluded under the agreement

Best Vision Accounting does not guarantee the outcome of any IRS matter. The IRS has final authority to determine tax, penalties, interest, credits and refunds.
