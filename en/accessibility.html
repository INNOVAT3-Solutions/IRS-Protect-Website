Last updated: October 9, 2026

Our commitment

Best Vision Accounting wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use the IRS Protect Plus website. Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, in both Spanish and English.

What we have done

  • We tested the Site with the automated axe-core tool on desktop and mobile, and fixed the color-contrast and accessible-name issues it found.
  • Forms can be completed with a keyboard alone, every field has a visible label, and errors are announced to screen readers.
  • Informative images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • The Site respects your device’s “reduce motion” setting and supports text zoom.

Known limitations

  • Some links lead to PDF documents on IRS.gov and other external sites that we do not control.
  • Automated testing cannot catch every issue; we keep reviewing the Site.

Feedback and help

If you run into an accessibility barrier or need information in another format, let us know and we will help you another way, for example by phone.
