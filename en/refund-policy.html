Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Membership Agreement Terms of Service Privacy Policy Cookie Policy Refunds and Cancellation Accessibility

This policy summarizes the cancellation and refund terms in section 9 of the Membership Agreement. If anything here differs from the Agreement, the Agreement controls.

1. Free trial, billing and automatic renewal

The IRS Protect Plus membership starts with a 30-day free trial. A payment card is collected at enrollment, but nothing is charged during the trial. The membership costs $19.99 a month after the trial: when the trial ends (day 31), the first $19.99 charge is made automatically to the payment method you provided, and the membership then renews automatically every month at $19.99 until you cancel.

2. How to cancel

You can cancel at any time, with no penalty:

  • online, in your billing portal: sign in with the email you enrolled with and you’ll get a link to cancel or update your card;
  • by email to info@bvaccounting.com;
  • by phone at (305) 220-9616, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm; or
  • through our Contact page, choosing the “Billing or cancellation” topic.

If you cancel during the 30-day free trial, you are not charged. Include your name and the email you used to enroll. Cancelling stops future charges.

3. Refunds

  • The first 30 days of membership are a free trial: no membership fee is paid during that period.
  • After the trial, fees paid during the first 30 days of paid membership (from the first charge) may be refundable if you have not requested, received or initiated any benefit under the program.
  • After those first 30 days of paid membership, membership fees already paid are non-refundable, except where otherwise required by law.

To request a refund, contact us using any of the methods above.

4. What happens to your benefits when you cancel

  • Cancellation ends your eligibility for future benefits.
  • If you cancel or your membership becomes inactive while Best Vision Accounting is handling a covered matter, Best Vision may discontinue the representation and other benefits, subject to applicable professional and legal requirements.
  • Re-enrolling after cancelling does not restore coverage for an IRS matter that arose before re-enrollment.
