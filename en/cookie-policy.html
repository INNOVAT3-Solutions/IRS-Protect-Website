1. Summary

The IRS Protect Plus website (the “Site”), operated by Best Vision Accounting, LLC, uses a small number of cookies and browser storage to remember your language and to measure, in aggregate, how the Site is used. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles about you or to sell information. This Policy explains what is stored in your browser, which third-party services are involved and how you can control it.

Your language preference stays on your device and is not sent to us. Microsoft does not publish cookie durations; the Clarity cookies above are commonly reported as 1 year (_clck) and 1 day (_clsk). You can delete or block these cookies in your browser settings; the Site will keep working. For how Microsoft uses its cookies, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

3. Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and Vercel Speed Insights to measure Site performance. According to Vercel’s documentation, Web Analytics does not use cookies: it identifies visits with a value derived from the request that resets every day, and discards the session after 24 hours. Speed Insights records anonymous performance measurements that, according to Vercel, are not tied to any visitor or IP address. We see only aggregated data, such as pages visited, referring site, browser and device type, and approximate location (country, region and city).

We also use Microsoft Clarity, a Microsoft Corporation service, to understand how the Site is used: anonymous session recordings, heatmaps and aggregated data. Clarity records activity on this Site (pages viewed, clicks and scrolling), not on other sites. What you type into form fields and dropdowns is always masked, and we also mark the areas that show personal data (for example, the names of the files you upload and the confirmation messages) so they are not recorded. Stripe's payment pages are not recorded. Microsoft keeps recordings for 30 days and aggregated data for up to 9 months, and processes that data under its Privacy Statement; Microsoft states that it acts as a data controller for Clarity data.

4. Third-party services

  • Behavioral analytics: Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation), described in section 3. Microsoft may set its own cookies, as explained in section 2.
  • Payment: when you pay for your membership, you enter your details and payment on a Stripe page. Stripe may use its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud, under its own privacy and cookie policies.
  • External links: the Site links to sites such as IRS.gov. Those sites have their own policies.

The Site does not include third-party ads, social media buttons, maps or embedded videos.

5. Cookie consent

We understand that neither U.S. federal law nor Florida law requires a consent banner for these analytics cookies, so we do not show one. Clarity's first-party cookies (_clck and _clsk) are used only to count visits and record anonymous sessions on this Site. Microsoft may use its own cookies under its Privacy Statement. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Microsoft Clarity requires a consent signal and does not record those visitors without a valid one. You can delete or block cookies in your browser at any time (section 2).

6. Do Not Track

We do not track your activity across other websites. Clarity recordings are limited to your activity on this Site, and Microsoft may use its own cookies under its Privacy Statement. The Site works the same way if your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal.
