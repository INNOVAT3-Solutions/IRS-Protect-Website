IRS Protect and IRS Protect Plus are membership programs offered by Best Vision Accounting. They are not insurance. Benefits consist of professional services and, for IRS Protect Plus, eligible IRS liability assistance, each subject to the terms, eligibility requirements, limitations and exclusions below.

1. The parties and this Agreement

This Membership Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between you (the “Member”, “you”) and Best Vision Accounting (“Best Vision”, “we”, “us”). It governs your membership in IRS Protect or IRS Protect Plus (each a “Program”). By enrolling, paying a membership fee, or requesting or using a Program benefit, you agree to this Agreement, our Terms of Service and our Privacy Policy.

2. The Programs are not insurance

The Programs are membership programs. They are not insurance policies, warranties, or guarantees, and Best Vision is not an insurer. Program benefits are professional tax representation services performed by Best Vision and, for IRS Protect Plus, limited assistance toward eligible additional federal tax liability, each as described in this Agreement.

3. Eligibility and the notice-date rule

An IRS matter is eligible for Program benefits only if your membership was active and in good standing on the date printed on the first IRS notice relating to that matter. A notice dated August 1 is not eligible under a membership that begins August 2, even if you opened the envelope later.

“Good standing” means all membership fees due have been paid and you are in compliance with this Agreement. Your membership must remain active and in good standing until the matter is resolved for benefits to continue. Best Vision may verify eligibility using the notice, the applicable federal tax return and any other documents it reasonably requests.

Benefits are provided per taxpayer for the taxpayer(s) named on the membership at enrollment, for their individual federal income tax matters.

4. IRS Protect benefits

IRS Protect provides up to $5,000 in professional tax representation services per taxpayer for eligible IRS matters, consisting of:

  • Notice and correspondence review. Reviewing IRS notices and correspondence, the applicable federal tax return, and supporting documents.
  • Responses to the IRS. Preparing responses and communicating with the IRS on the Member’s behalf.
  • Examinations and audits. Helping during an IRS examination or audit, with professional representation before the IRS when authorized by you (for example through IRS Form 2848, Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative).
  • Collection and resolution matters. Assistance with eligible IRS collection or resolution matters.

The $5,000 represents the value of professional services provided by Best Vision at its standard rates. It is not cash, is not paid to you, and does not pay your tax bill, penalties or interest. Services beyond the $5,000 limit, if any, are available at Best Vision’s standard rates by separate engagement.

5. IRS Protect Plus benefit

IRS Protect Plus includes everything in IRS Protect. In addition, if a qualifying IRS examination, audit or covered adjustment on an eligible matter results in additional eligible federal tax owed, Best Vision may provide up to $2,000 toward that eligible additional federal tax liability. The Plus benefit is assistance toward eligible additional federal tax liability arising from a covered matter. It is not a general credit toward anything owed. Among other exclusions, the Plus benefit does not pay:

  • Taxes already owed before membership;
  • Tax originally reported as due on your return;
  • Pre-existing IRS liabilities;
  • Matters arising from notices dated before enrollment;
  • Liabilities from fraudulent or intentionally false returns;
  • Criminal tax matters;
  • Estimated tax payments;
  • Government filing or processing fees;
  • Amounts unrelated to the covered matter;
  • Amounts exceeding $2,000; or
  • Penalties and interest, unless Best Vision agrees otherwise in writing.

The Plus benefit is limited to $2,000 per membership in total, regardless of the number of matters, tax years or taxpayers. Best Vision determines whether a matter and amount qualify, based on the IRS determination and the documents you provide.

6. What is not covered

The following are excluded from all Programs:

  • Fraud and intentional tax evasion;
  • Criminal tax investigations and criminal tax matters;
  • IRS matters whose first notice is dated before your membership start date (pre-enrollment matters);
  • Intentionally false or incomplete returns, and intentionally concealed information;
  • Representation in any court, including the United States Tax Court;
  • State, local and non-U.S. tax matters;
  • Preparation of original or amended tax returns, except as Best Vision determines is necessary to resolve an eligible matter;
  • Matters in which you do not provide the notice, the applicable return, requested documents or required authorizations.

7. Your responsibilities

  • Provide every IRS notice or letter promptly after you receive it and before any deadline stated in it, using the Submit a notice page or another method we designate.
  • Provide the applicable federal tax return, supporting documents and any other information Best Vision reasonably requests, completely and truthfully.
  • Sign IRS authorizations (such as Form 2848 or Form 8821) when needed so Best Vision can communicate with the IRS for you.
  • Respond to Best Vision’s requests in a timely manner and tell us about any communication you have with the IRS about the matter.
  • Keep your membership active and in good standing until the matter is resolved.

Benefits may be delayed, limited or denied if these responsibilities are not met, or if information you provide is false or incomplete.

8. No guarantee of outcome

Best Vision does not guarantee the outcome of any IRS dispute. The IRS determines federal tax liabilities, penalties, interest, credits and refunds. Best Vision will use reasonable professional judgment in providing the services described in this Agreement.

9. Fees, billing and renewal

Membership fees are $10.99 per month for IRS Protect and $19.99 per month for IRS Protect Plus, plus applicable taxes. Fees are billed monthly in advance to the payment method you provide, and your membership renews automatically each month until cancelled. You authorize Best Vision and its payment processor to charge the recurring fee. If a payment fails, your membership may lapse and matters arising during the lapse will not be eligible. Best Vision may change fees with at least 30 days’ notice; changes apply to your next billing period.

10. Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel at any time by contacting Best Vision. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and matters whose first notice is dated after that date are not eligible. Fees paid during the first 30 days of membership may be refundable if you have not requested, received or initiated a Program benefit. After the first 30 days, paid fees generally are not refundable.

Best Vision may suspend or terminate your membership for non-payment, fraud, misrepresentation, abusive conduct toward staff, or a material breach of this Agreement. Benefits for matters in progress may cease at termination.

11. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Best Vision’s total liability to you arising out of or relating to the Programs or this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, is limited to the membership fees you paid in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim. Best Vision is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for any tax, penalty or interest assessed by the IRS, except for the Plus benefit expressly described in Section 5. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable law, including for professional negligence to the extent required by the rules governing tax practitioners.

12. Changes to the Programs or this Agreement

Best Vision may update this Agreement or the Programs. We will post the updated Agreement on this page with a new “Last updated” date and, for material changes, notify you by email or through the website before they take effect. Your continued membership after the effective date constitutes acceptance. Changes do not reduce benefits for eligible matters already submitted.

13. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Best Vision Accounting maintains its principal office, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Before starting any formal proceeding, you agree to contact Best Vision and attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute for at least 30 days.

14. General

This Agreement, together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, is the entire agreement between you and Best Vision regarding the Programs. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. You may not assign your membership; Best Vision may assign this Agreement to a successor of its business. Notices to you will be sent to the email address on your membership.
